## Apple App Store

**Subtitle (max 30):** Write and schedule your posts

**Promotional text (max 170):** Research a niche, write the script, schedule posts for six platforms, send them to any app by webhook, and check every post against each platform's rules.

**Description (max 4000):**

Nexus Pulse is a content studio and scheduler for creators who run channels without being on camera.

It does four jobs in one place:

RESEARCH
See which niches pay the most per view in the Niche Lab, then ask for a ranked report with story ideas and a 30-day plan.

WRITE
Story Studio turns an idea into a timed script: hook, scenes, on-screen text, visuals to source, title options, thumbnail text, description and a fact-check list. Posts for X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok are written in each platform's native format.

SCHEDULE AND POST
Drag posts onto a weekly calendar, or press "Fill my week". Posts publish automatically where the platform allows it: Facebook Pages, X, LinkedIn and Instagram. Where a platform doesn't allow it, you get copy-ready text instead, and the app says why.

CONNECT YOUR APPS
Link accounts with each platform's own sign-in page. Nexus Pulse never sees your password. Use the webhook connection to send posts to Zapier, Make, n8n, Slack, Discord or your own server, so they reach whatever app you use.

KEEP YOUR ACCOUNTS SAFE
Every post is checked against platform rules before it goes out. A kill switch, daily limits and quiet hours keep posting under your control. Posts can wait for your approval.

WHAT TO KNOW
- You need accounts on the platforms you connect. Some platforms charge for API access or require their own app review; the app shows what each connection allows.
- Writing is done by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic. Review what it writes before you publish; you are responsible for what goes out under your name.
- Plans: Free, Creator, Pro and Unlimited. Subscribe on our website. Prices are shown on the Plans page.

**What's New (first release):** First release. Research niches, write scripts and posts for six platforms, schedule them, and connect any app with webhooks.

**App Review notes (paste into App Store Connect → App Review Information):**
- Demo account: create one in App Store Connect and fill in the email and password here before submitting.
- The app is a native shell around our hosted web app, so every screen reflects the live service.
- Purchases: the iOS app does not sell subscriptions. Purchase buttons are hidden when the app detects it is running natively (user agent `NexusPulseApp`). Existing subscribers sign in with the account they created on the website.
- Account deletion is in Settings → Account → Delete account.
- AI-generated content: text is generated by Anthropic's Claude API and shown to the user for review before publishing.
- Third-party sign-in pages (X, Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok) open in the system browser.

**Age rating:** Answer the questionnaire honestly. Mature mode (X only, non-explicit, off by default) and user-generated/AI text content will probably lead to a 17+ rating. Let Apple's questionnaire decide.

**Privacy "nutrition label" (App Store Connect → App Privacy). Check these against what the app really collects before submitting:**
- Contact info: email address (account), linked to the user.
- User content: posts, scripts, uploaded media, linked to the user.
- Identifiers / usage data: only if you add analytics. The app has none today.
- Purchases: handled on the web by Stripe; no payment details are stored by the app.
- No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
