# Ghost Post — marketing copy

Rules used for everything below: it describes what the app does today; it has no income promises, no invented
numbers, no testimonials and no "guaranteed" claims. Add real numbers and real customer quotes only after you have them.

## One line
Ghost Post is a content studio and scheduler for faceless creators: research a niche, write the script, schedule the posts, and check every one against each platform's rules.

## Two sentences
Ghost Post writes posts and video scripts in each platform's native format, then schedules them to X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok. Where a platform allows automatic posting it posts for you; where it doesn't, you get copy-ready text and a plain explanation of why.

## What it is, in plain words
You pick a niche and a topic. Ghost Post, using Claude (an AI model from Anthropic), writes a script with a hook, timed scenes, on-screen text, visuals to find, title options and a fact-check list. It turns the idea into posts for each platform and puts them on a weekly calendar. Before anything goes out, a rules check looks for things that get accounts in trouble: duplicate posts, engagement bait, financial guarantees, medical claims and unapproved links. You stay in control with a kill switch, daily limits, quiet hours and an optional approval step.

## Who it is for
- Creators who run channels without appearing on camera.
- Solo marketers who post on several platforms and want one calendar.
- Anyone who would rather edit a draft than start from a blank page.

## Who it is not for
- People who want a guaranteed income. Nothing here can promise views or revenue.
- People who want to post without reading what goes out. You are responsible for what is published under your name.

## Landing-page copy

**Headline:** Write it. Schedule it. Keep your accounts in good standing.

**Sub-headline:** Research a niche, get a script that holds attention, and schedule posts for six platforms, from one calendar.

**Three blocks**
1. *Research first.* The Niche Lab shows revenue-per-view data by niche and ranks sub-niches for you, with story ideas and a 30-day plan.
2. *Written for each platform.* A thread isn't a carousel, and a Reel script isn't a LinkedIn post. Each one is written in the format that platform uses.
3. *Connect the apps you already use.* Sign in with each platform's own page, or send posts to any tool through a webhook. We never see your passwords.

**Safety block:** Every post is checked before it goes out. A kill switch stops everything with one tap.

**Pricing block** (from the app's Plans page; confirm before publishing):

**FAQ**
- *Does it post for me?* On Facebook Pages, X, LinkedIn, Instagram and webhooks, yes, once you connect the account. YouTube and TikTok are copy-ready because of those platforms' rules.
- *Do you see my password?* No. You sign in on the platform's own page and approve permissions there. Only an access token is stored, encrypted.
- *Who writes the content?* Claude, an AI model from Anthropic. You review it. You decide what is published.
- *Will it make me money?* We can't promise that. It saves time on research, writing and scheduling.
- *Can I delete my data?* Yes: Settings → Account → Delete account, or see the data deletion page.
