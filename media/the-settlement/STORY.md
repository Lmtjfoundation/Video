# The Settlement: Story Bible

*A Houston family drama in 10 episodes. Fiction.*

**Logline:** Tasha pulled Marcus out of the streets and built him a clean life. Twelve years later she wants something
of her own, and the man from his past who knows exactly how to use that is draining them dry. Then a crash on
Telephone Road meant for Marcus turns into a twenty-million-dollar settlement, in her name only.

**The question that sells it:** She betrayed him. She also saved him. Whose side are you on?

---

## The people

**Marcus Grant (42).** Ran the streets of Houston's Southside in his early twenties with his best friend Dre. Tasha
got him out: trade school, an AC license, a repair business in both their names. Twelve years clean. Works sixty
hours a week in the Texas heat so the house is always cool when he comes home. He gave her every key, every password,
every dollar, because she earned it. He never talks about who he used to be.

**Tasha Grant (40).** The reason Marcus is alive. She keeps the books, the bills, the accounts and the business.
Smart, proud, tired. She has spent twelve years being the woman who saved him, and somewhere in there she stopped
being anything else. She wants something of her own: the boutique she never opened, to be seen, a life that isn't
about rescuing him. That hunger is the crack someone slips through.

**Dre (43).** Marcus's running partner and the brother he chose. When they got caught, Dre took the charge for both
of them and did nine years. Marcus walked, because Tasha had already pulled him out. Dre comes home to find Marcus
with a house, a business and a wife. He doesn't come back for Marcus. He comes back for what Marcus has, and he gets
it through Tasha by telling her exactly what she has been waiting to hear.

**Kev (50s).** Tow-truck driver who knew both men back in the day. Parks at the light on Telephone Road most nights.
His dashcam is always running. He has never trusted Dre.

---

## The series: 10 episodes

Each episode is about 3–5 minutes, vertical, and ends on a hook.

### Episode 1: Twelve Years
- **Cold open, Houston 2012.** Marcus at 22 on a Southside corner with Dre. Sirens. Tasha pulls up: *"Get in this
  car, or I'm done with you."* Marcus gets in. Dre doesn't, and does nine years for both of them.
- **Twelve years later.** Marcus gives his wife every key, every password, every dollar. He fixes air conditioners
  sixty hours a week. Tasha keeps the books. *TASHA: "Baby, I handle the money. That's what I'm good at."*
- **Tasha wants more.** After he falls asleep she draws dresses for a boutique she never opened. Nobody ever asked what
  she wanted. *Somebody was about to.*
- **The money moves.** The bank letter. A $31,000 card in his name. *MARCUS: "Then why is the bank calling me?"* A
  loan against his mother's house with a signature that isn't his. The missing birthdays.
- **Hook:** a second phone in a shoebox behind her heels. One contact. One letter: **D.**

### Episode 2: One Contact
- Dre walks out of prison after nine years. Nobody is waiting. He sees Marcus's truck with the business name on the
  door and a house with the lights on.
- Flashback: the 2012 arrest, and the promise Marcus made Dre through the glass that he never kept.
- Dre finds Tasha, not Marcus: at the bank, alone. "An old friend of Marcus's." He asks her what *she* wants. Nobody
  has asked her that in twelve years.
- **Hook:** Marcus calls the number on the second phone. Someone picks up and says nothing. Then a laugh he knows.

### Episode 3: The Boutique
- Tasha's side. Dre shows her an empty storefront on Almeda and says it's hers. Attention becomes an affair.
- The money for the lease comes from Marcus's accounts, then the credit card, then the loan. Every dollar goes to Dre's
  "investment." None of it reaches the boutique.
- When she pulls back, Dre tells her about a 2012 case that was never closed, and what one phone call could do to
  Marcus.
- **Hook:** Tasha realizes she isn't Dre's partner. She's his account.

### Episode 4: Static
- Marcus investigates quietly: statements she swore she shredded, the mail before she gets home.
- He finds a life-insurance policy on himself, doubled six months ago. Beneficiary: Tasha.
- The old baby monitor upstairs, still plugged in, in a room where nobody sleeps. Through the static, her voice, then
  Dre's.
- **Hook:** *DRE: "Ten o'clock. Telephone Road. Make sure he drives."*

### Episode 5: Ten O'Clock
- The day of. Tasha asks Marcus to run an errand at ten. He says yes, and watches her face.
- Rain so hard the wipers can't keep up. Marcus never goes; he sits in his driveway in the dark and waits.
- Tasha, sick with guilt, drives to Telephone Road to call it off. *She did.*
- Kev's tow truck is parked at the light, dashcam running.
- **Hook:** the truck meant for Marcus finds her instead.

### Episode 6: The Fourth Floor
- Three weeks on the fourth floor. She lives, barely. Marcus sleeps in the chair by her bed.
- A detective asks why Marcus stayed home that night.
- Dre visits, signing in as her cousin.
- **Hook:** Tasha wakes and her first words to Marcus are *"You were supposed to be there."*

### Episode 7: Twenty Million
- The lawyers come fast. The trucking company settles: **twenty million dollars, in her name only.**
- On paper she's the victim. On paper he's the husband who stayed home. His mother's house goes into foreclosure.
- Dre wants his cut, and this time he isn't asking.
- **Hook:** Tasha has to choose: the man from the streets who owns her secrets, or the husband she saved and then
  betrayed.

### Episode 8: The Tape
- Kev finds Marcus at a job site. He knew them both back in the day, and he never trusted Dre.
- His dashcam caught every second: the car, the truck, and the driver on the phone right before the crash.
- He didn't take it to the police. He brought it to Marcus.
- **Hook:** *KEV: "I kept the tape. All of it. Every second."* The number on the driver's phone is Dre's.

### Episode 9: Old Ways
- Marcus's old crew offers to handle Dre the way it used to get handled. For one night he's 22 again.
- Tasha, home from the hospital, tells him everything: the boutique, the money, Dre, the plan, and that she went to
  stop it.
- **Hook:** *MARCUS: "I heard everything, Tasha."* He shows her the baby monitor. She shows him what she recorded
  on Dre.

### Episode 10: The Settlement
- Two secret recordings, one settlement, and one meeting with Dre, who thinks he's getting paid.
- The tape and Tasha's recording go to the police together. Dre is arrested on Telephone Road.
- Tasha takes a plea for the fraud so Marcus's name stays clean. The settlement pays off his mother's house.
- **Final image** mirrors 2012: twelve years ago she said "get in this car" and saved him. Now she's in the back of a
  police car, and he's the one standing on the curb.
- **Three people. Two recordings. One settlement. And only one of them walks away.** *Whose side are you on?*

## What stays from the original
Everything: the twelve years, the keys and passwords, the bank letter, the $31,000 card, the loan on his mother's
house, the missing birthdays, the shoebox phone with one contact, the baby monitor, Telephone Road at ten o'clock, the
crash, three weeks in the hospital, the $20 million settlement in her name only, Kev's dashcam, and "Whose side are
you on?"

## What's new
- Marcus's street past, and Tasha as the one who got him out.
- Dre as Marcus's old partner who did nine years for both of them.
- Tasha wanting something of her own, and Dre using that to drain them for money.
- Tasha trying to call it off on Telephone Road, so the crash is the price of her trying to undo it.
